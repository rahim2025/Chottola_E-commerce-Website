## ⚠️ Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

- ❌ Duplicate meta descriptions
- ❌ Keyword stuffing
- ❌ Thin/copied content
- ❌ Broken internal links
- ❌ Missing alt text on images
- ❌ Ignoring mobile experience
- ❌ Slow page load times
- ❌ Over-optimization for one keyword
- ❌ Neglecting user intent
- ❌ Inconsistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone)

## 💡 Pro Tips for Better SEO

1. **User Intent First**: Write for users, not search engines
2. **Long-tail Keywords**: Easier to rank, higher conversion rate
3. **Content Freshness**: Update old content regularly
4. **E-E-A-T**: Show Expertise, Experience, Authority, Trustworthiness
5. **Internal Linking**: Strategic links between related content
6. **Mobile Optimization**: 60%+ of traffic is mobile
7. **Site Speed**: Every 1s delay = 7% conversion decrease
8. **Core Web Vitals**: Critical ranking factor
9. **Reviews & Ratings**: Social proof and schema opportunities
10. **Local SEO**: Important for Bangladesh-based business
